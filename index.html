Suggested Learning Path

A practical order for building the subject from fundamentals to circuits.

01 · Number Systems
Binary → decimal → octal → hexadecimal and complements.
02 · Logic Gates
Truth tables, symbols, Boolean expressions and universal gates.
03 · Combinational Logic
Adders, multiplexers, demultiplexers, encoders and decoders.
04 · Sequential Logic
Latches, flip-flops, registers and counters.
05 · Practice
Use the simulators, formula sheet and quiz to verify your understanding.
